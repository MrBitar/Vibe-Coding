If you're in danger right now, please reach out immediately

  • US: Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), available 24/7.
  • US (text-based): Text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line).
  • Outside the US: Find a local crisis line via the International Association for Suicide Prevention's crisis center directory.
  • If you or someone else is in immediate physical danger, call your local emergency number (e.g. 911 in the US) now.
