Our Story

Where and when Restaurant Makki caught fire.

Founded in Jibsheet, 2023

Jibsheet, Main Street

Restaurant Makki was founded in 2023 in Jibsheet, Lebanon, when Abbass Makki, a charcoal-grill cook who had spent a decade perfecting his marinade at a small roadside stall, opened the first Restaurant Makki storefront.

The family recipe — garlic, lemon and a secret spice blend rubbed into the chicken the night before grilling — became known neighborhood-wide, and the little stall grew into the restaurant it is today.

Three years later, the Makki family still runs Restaurant Makki, still grilling over real charcoal, still using Abbass's original marinade.
